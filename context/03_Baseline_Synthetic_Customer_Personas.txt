1. Purpose and responsible use
These six fictional personas are designed to create meaningful differences in a cross-industry demo. They are not statistically representative people and must not be presented as real research. Their traits, metrics, stories, and evidence are synthetic. The set is intentionally based on decision styles, relationship stages, and experience needs rather than protected characteristics or industry-specific product ownership.
DESIGN RULE Use personas as hypotheses grounded in illustrative evidence. Show confidence, provenance, versioning, and limitations wherever a persona produces an answer or prediction.

10. Versioning and refresh conventions
Baseline version: v1.0 for every persona.
Dynamic traits may move by small increments after a learning event; stable goals should not change after a single campaign.
Every proposed update includes triggering evidence, alternative explanations, confidence impact, reviewer, and approval state.
A single campaign result can propose a change, but the UI should signal when corroboration is needed.
The demo should include one approved update and one rejected update to demonstrate governance.
