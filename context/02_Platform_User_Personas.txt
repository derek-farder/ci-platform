1. How to use this document
These are product user personas, not synthetic customers. They guide navigation, workflow priority, permissions, terminology, and demo storytelling. The MVP should optimize for the first three while remaining credible to the others.

9. Permission model for the demo
Viewer: Explore personas, read studies, and view journey and learning results.
Contributor: Ask questions, create panels, build studies, and draft activation briefs.
Approver: Approve activation handoffs and persona update proposals.
Administrator cues are out of scope beyond a visible role label and audit history.
