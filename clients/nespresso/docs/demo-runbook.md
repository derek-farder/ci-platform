# ICC Synthetic Demo Runbook

Target duration: 5–7 minutes. Audience: internal Innovation Manager, CRC Agent Tier 1, and Market Leader. Keep the one-line synthetic footer visible; open “Assumptions & method” when asked about a source or caveat.

1. **Choose a door (30 seconds).** Start with Innovation Advisor. Mention Marketing Advisor and Persona Advisor can enter the same flow with a different starter question.
2. **Ask (1 minute).** Show the grouped top contact reasons and relative priority score. The ranking is calculated from scenario inputs, not first-party operating data.
3. **Meet the cohort (1 minute).** Open Overdue Descaler; ask why the alert was left for later. Identify the persona voice badge and the evidence chips.
4. **Test the idea (1.5 minutes).** Set a nudge delay after an ignored descale alert. Move uptake/effect, compare the baseline and with-nudge monthly lines, and switch between cohort scale and 100,000. Show the avoided-contact share and capsule-value sensitivity.
5. **Decide (1 minute).** Review the ranked backlog and quarterly roadmap strip. Adding the descaling guide changes only local demo state; no external backlog or roadmap is written.
6. **Zoom out (1 minute).** Open Subscription and Loyalty computed charts, then ask the five-cohort panel question and read the qualitative synthesis.
7. **Plug in data (30 seconds).** Show the source-system map and the proposed back-test of last quarter's top CRC contact reasons.
