# ICC Synthetic Demo Runbook

Target duration: 5–7 minutes. Audience: internal Innovation Manager, CRC Agent Tier 1, and Market Leader. Keep the one-line synthetic footer visible; open “Assumptions & method” when asked about a source or caveat.

1. **Choose a door (30 seconds).** Start with Innovation Advisor. Persona Advisor enters the same flow from one cohort. The lens is CX (NPS, effort, staying with Nespresso), not campaigns or cart conversion.
2. **Ask (1 minute).** Show the grouped pain points with effort (CES), NPS impact, and relative priority score. Contacts are a pain signal, not the goal. The ranking is calculated from scenario inputs, not first-party operating data.
3. **Meet the cohort (1 minute).** Open Overdue Descaler; ask why the alert was left for later. Identify the persona voice badge and the evidence chips.
4. **Test an experience change (about 2.5 minutes).** Choose one of three experience changes (clearer alert, guided in-app descaling, proactive check-in), run the staged panel, and switch among variants. On each cohort card, point to the stance split across its three persona variations: owners in one cohort differ, and that spread is what a 10-person study can miss. Show the discover → understand → complete funnel. Completion and risk reduction come from provisional attributes, never persona text. Review the synthesis, then show the 12-month NPS shift, direct repurchase change, and contacts avoided, with capsule value as secondary business context. Manual sliders stay collapsed under “Override assumptions.”
5. **Decide (1 minute).** Review the ranked backlog and quarterly roadmap strip. Adding the descaling guide changes only local demo state; no external backlog or roadmap is written.
6. **Zoom out (1 minute).** Open the NPS-by-cohort and “Staying with Nespresso” computed charts, then ask the five-cohort panel question and read the qualitative synthesis.
7. **Plug in data (30 seconds).** Show the source-system map (including NPS/VoC and app analytics) and the proposed back-test of last quarter's top contact reasons and NPS by pain point.

Reset returns to the three entry doors. Interim theme and supplied logo are approved for this internal demo; confirm usage rules with Vincent before external distribution. Do not describe synthetic data, the scenario simulation, or persona voice as actual Nespresso research.

No live LLM is used in the demo. In production, a researcher agent and synthesizer agent (for example, using Bedrock/AgentCore) could generate grounded reactions and synthesis, while deterministic analytics continue to own all numeric outputs.
