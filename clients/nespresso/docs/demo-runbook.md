# ICC Synthetic Demo Runbook

Target duration: 5–7 minutes when presented. The demo is also shared with the Nespresso innovation team to explore on their own; the Welcome page explains the guided path, what is an example, and what is synthetic. Audience: Innovation Manager, CRC Agent Tier 1, and Market Leader. Keep the one-line synthetic footer visible; open “Assumptions & method” when asked about a source or caveat.

**Entry:** `clients/nespresso/` opens the Overview landing page ([`../index.html`](../index.html)); “See it in action” opens the demo, and the demo sidebar links back to Overview. The Overview copy comes from the user-supplied *Agent brief: Nespresso ICC v0.4, landing page + CX measures* (2026-10-15), including the From → To wording from Diana’s deck (slide 4). It describes the future state; the PoC is a small Machine Assistance test run. The 7-step process is labelled as our understanding of a typical process, to be tailored with the client. Print / Save as PDF gives one section per page with the process shown as a full Today vs. With the ICC table.

1. **Welcome (30 seconds).** Point out the six-step guided path and the “What is real and what is not” panel. Start the guided path (Innovation Advisor). Persona Advisor starts from the customer and joins at step 2 (Cohorts). The lens is CX (NPS, effort, staying with Nespresso), not campaigns or cart conversion.
2. **Ask (1 minute).** Optionally open “Workarounds & unmet needs” to show example themes combined across contact notes, NPS comments, reviews, social posts, and machine events. Then show the grouped pain points with effort (CES), NPS impact, and relative priority score. Contacts are a pain signal, not the goal. The ranking is calculated from scenario inputs, not first-party operating data.
3. **Meet the cohort (1 minute).** Open Overdue Descaler; ask why the alert was left for later. Identify the persona voice badge and the evidence chips.
4. **Test an experience change (about 2.5 minutes).** Choose one of three experience changes (clearer alert, guided in-app descaling, proactive check-in), run the staged panel, and switch among variants. On each cohort card, point to the stance split across its three persona variations and their head counts (e.g. 900 / 1,350 / 750 of 3,000 Overdue Descalers): the variations stand in for asking everyone in the cohort, owners in one cohort differ, and that spread is what a 10-person study can miss. Each card also shows a simulated NPS direction and customer-effort rating. Show the discover → understand → complete funnel. Completion and risk reduction come from provisional attributes, never persona text. Review the synthesis, then show the 12-month NPS shift, direct repurchase change, and contacts avoided, with capsule value as secondary business context. Manual sliders stay collapsed under “Override assumptions.”
5. **Decide (1 minute).** Review the ranked backlog and quarterly roadmap strip. Adding the descaling guide changes only local demo state; no external backlog or roadmap is written.
6. **Zoom out (1 minute).** Open the NPS-by-cohort and “Staying with Nespresso” computed charts, then ask the five-cohort panel question and read the qualitative synthesis.
7. **Plug in data (30 seconds).** Show the source-system map (including NPS/VoC and app analytics) and the proposed back-test of last quarter's top contact reasons and NPS by pain point.

Reset returns to the Welcome page. The interim theme and supplied logo are approved for client use (2026-10-15). Do not describe synthetic data, the scenario simulation, or persona voice as actual Nespresso research.

No live LLM is used in the demo. In production, a researcher agent and synthesizer agent (for example, using Bedrock/AgentCore) could generate grounded reactions and synthesis, while deterministic analytics continue to own all numeric outputs.
