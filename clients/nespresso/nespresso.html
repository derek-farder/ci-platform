Assumptions & method

This internal concept uses synthetic personas, cohort aggregates, and scenario assumptions. No Nespresso customer data or live integrations are connected.

Simulation method

Monthly issue risk × nudge uptake × effect size; delay starts relative to an ignored descale alert. The sensitivity band is not a statistical confidence interval.

Revenue assumption

Illustrative €0.65 per capsule midpoint, with €0.50–€0.85 price sensitivity. Not Nespresso net revenue. Market mix is unselected; replace with actual average selling price when available.

Sanity check: 54 capsules/month × 12 × €0.65 ≈ €421 per customer per year.

Scenario rules

Planted relationships are hypotheses for this demo, not causal findings. Confirm sources, market, consent, and measure definitions before any first-party pilot.
