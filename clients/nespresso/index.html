INNOVATION COMMAND CENTER · CONCEPT

Test the idea before you build it.

The Innovation Command Center helps Nespresso’s innovation team find the experience problems that matter most, try many solutions on a panel of synthetic customers built from our own data, and bring stronger evidence to every go/no-go decision.

See it in action How it works
WHAT THE ICC IS

A flight simulator for customer-experience ideas.

Think of it as a flight simulator for customer-experience ideas. Pilots still fly real planes; they just arrive having practised the hard parts.

Innovation Advisor

Finds where to focus.

It reads service, machine and research data plus public studies, and points to the pain points most worth solving.

Synthetic customer panel

Tests ideas at scale.

Hundreds of synthetic respondents, built from Nespresso’s own customer segments, react to concepts, prototypes and delivery options, and explain why.

Evidence and learning

Turns answers into decisions.

Results feed the stage gates and backlog, and every real-world result makes the panel more accurate.

THE FUNNEL

More ideas get a fair look. Fewer weak ones reach a prototype.

Widths are relative and illustrative, not measured.

WHY YOU CAN TRUST IT

Synthetic for direction, real customers for decisions.

Built from your data

Personas come from Nespresso’s own segments, user stories and service data, not generic internet profiles.

Every answer is labeled

Observed, simulated and persona voice are always distinct, so you know what kind of evidence you’re looking at.

Checked against what you already know

Before it’s trusted on new ideas, the panel must reproduce pain points you’ve already found.

People decide

Every gate decision stays with the team; the panel informs, it doesn’t approve.

Confidence you can see, and it grows

Each result shows its confidence level, and real-world outcomes recalibrate the panel over time.

FROM POC TO A NEW WAY OF INNOVATING

Start small, prove it, then scale.

  1. Today

    Sequential and research-limited

    Prototype-first. Testing is slow, so few ideas are explored.

  2. The PoC · a small test run

    Machine Assistance

    Uses the segments, user stories and telemetry you already have. Proves the panel is credible and makes the most of existing data.

    A few real concepts, such as setup guidance, maintenance help and connected-machine ideas, run through the full loop.

  3. Scaled ICC

    A new way of innovating

    More pillars: Subscription, Loyalty, Conversational, Farm-to-cup. Continuous opportunity triage, research focused where it matters most, and a learning capability that compounds.
