# ICC Synthetic Demo Runbook

Target duration: 5–7 minutes. Audience: internal Innovation Manager, CRC Agent Tier 1, and Market Leader. All output is illustrative; this is not a Nespresso customer analysis.

1. **Ask (about 1 minute).** Open `nespresso.html`. Choose “Which machine issues drive the most CRC contacts, and which are preventable?” Explain that the counts and priority proxy come from fabricated fixture values. Clarify that the proxy ranks contact volume × preventability × retention impact; it is not an impact forecast.
2. **Meet the cohort (about 1 minute).** Open Overdue Descaler. Point out its 30% synthetic share and fixture signals. Read the illustrative persona voice as role-play, not a transcript or statistical result. Identify the planted >60-day descale rule as an assumption.
3. **Test the idea (about 2 minutes).** Simulate a day-75 app nudge across 12 monthly steps. Adjust uptake and issue-risk reduction and update the scenario. Show CRC contacts, lapse, capsule-volume outputs, assumptions, and the simple ±12% sensitivity band. State that the band is not a statistical confidence interval and the result is not a forecast.
4. **Decide (about 1 minute).** Review the Feature Card and provisional priority score. Explain production data needs and that Nessy Chapter 1 fit/weights require stakeholder agreement. “Add to ranked demo backlog” only changes local demo state; it does not create an external ticket or roadmap commitment.
5. **Zoom out (about 1 minute).** Open Subscription and Loyalty previews. Each has one canned question and a limited answer; neither is live analysis. Return to Machine Assistance or reset the demo.

Before presenting, confirm the market, branding, feature destination, scoring rubric, and asset approval. Do not describe any synthetic share, count, planted rule, persona voice, or projected result as real customer evidence.
